# Brainstorm: refinements and new directions (17 Oct 2026)

Low bar, informal. Inputs: the app on `main` (Should earns Want at 1:3, scene timer,
Today/Later lists, Fix timer, Supabase sync), the two-state spec, the design canvas
notes, and the kata board (kaizen, pre-flight checklist, Pomodoro, mobile vs desktop,
rename, Focus/Break wording, "work for X / rest for X", scene variations, break time
across midnight).

Observations from reading the code:

- Phase 4 of the spec (the **Days** history view) was never built. There is no way to
  look back at all.
- `sounds.ts` and `notifications.ts` were carried over but nothing calls them. The app
  is silent, even when Want runs into debt.
- On a desktop the app is a 28rem column in the middle of the screen.
- The scene takes `weather` but nothing passes it; the moon never changes phase.

2. Want for "all I have": the intent defaults to the current balance.
3. Chime and notification when running Want crosses into debt.
4. "Clear debt in 18m of Should": show what it takes to get back to zero.
5. Projection: "at this pace you reach target at 16:40".
6. Carry the balance over midnight while a stint is running (old 7mfa card).
7. Bring back the ratio choice (Quarter / Third / Half) as a per-day pick.
8. Countdown display mode for the running stint when it has an intent.
9. Idle detection: "no input for 20 min, still on Should?" (prompt to Fix).
10. Tab title shows the clock and state, so it's visible from another tab.
11. Keyboard: S, W, Space to stop, F to fix.
12. Hold-to-stop to avoid accidental stops.

**Rituals and momentum (kaizen)** 13. Pre-flight checklist before the first Should of the day: clear desk, water, phone

## Pass 2: test against the principles

The spec's principles: awareness, not enforcement; one ratio; time derived, never
counted; stopped is rest and carries no guilt.

- **Cut or park as conflicting:** 7 (ratio modes, the spec chose one ratio on purpose),
  9 and 12 (nagging and friction), 21 (pricing Wants turns rest into a shop, which
  pushes guilt), 6 (the clipped day window already handles a stint across midnight;
  the balance resetting at the boundary is the stated design).
- **Merge:** 1 + 2 + 8 into one **intent** on the running stint. 3 belongs with it: both
  are "tell me when a line is crossed". 14 + 15 + 16 are the kaizen card and belong in
  the missing Days view, not on Today. 18 + 23 are one "closing" moment, worth doing
  after Days exists.
- **Cheap wins that fit as-is:** 4, 10, 11, 31.
- **Bigger, later:** 25 and 26 (scene), 33 (push needs a server piece), 35 and 36
  (better decided together at the end, as the rename card says), 37 (fun, needs a
  sharing model), 17 (a new `Recurrence` kind that must sync; small but touches the
  schema), 29.
- **Check:** 13 must stay optional and skippable, or it becomes enforcement. One tap
  "ready" and one tap "skip", once per day, on starting Should from rest.

## Pass 3: shape what gets built now

Picked for this round, all local-first and none touching the sync schema:

1. **Days view** (spec Phase 4, kaizen card). Third tab. Per-day Should, Want, ending
   balance, target hit. A five-week heatmap of Should time and a "showed up" streak
   (days with any Should; today counts once it has some, and a quiet today doesn't
   break the streak until it ends). Read-only for now; editing entries reuses `paint`
   later.
2. **Intent and chimes** (Pomodoro and "work for X" cards). When a stint is running,
   chips for "for 15 / 25 / 50 min" on Should, and "for 5 / 10 / all" on Want. The
   intent is per device and lives only while that stint runs. When it's reached:
   chime, notification, and the clock line says "25m done". Separately, Want crossing
   from credit into debt chimes once. Nothing stops or switches by itself.
3. **Debt hint**: under a negative balance, "18m of Should clears it".
4. **Pre-flight checklist** (0r7e). Edited in Settings, one line per step, empty by
   default so nothing changes for anyone who doesn't want it. When non-empty, the first
   Should start of the day opens a short sheet: tick steps, then Start, or Skip.
5. **Desktop layout** (zfbg). From `lg` up: two columns, timer sticky on the left,
   lists on the right, the Today/Later/Days bar centred under the lists column.
6. **Tab title and keys**: `▶ 12:04 Should · Third Time` while running; S, W, Space (stop)
   and F (fix) when focus isn't in a text field.

Left for later rounds: scene weather and moon, labels and rename, spaced repeats, the
closing ritual, web push, body-double mode, Days entry editing.
