Third Time — redesign mockups

A tabbed shell with the session pinned on top, routines flattened into boolean habits, and goals that accumulate toward an outcome instead of resetting each week. Dark theme shown throughout — the light palette carries over unchanged.

Decided

  • Tabs replace the accordion sections; session zone is pinned above them
  • One boolean habit per step — flat list, no groups
  • Habit rows read out as recent-history dots (last 7), no streak counts
  • Goals are cumulative: measure = Time / Count / Open, milestones optional on any
  • Goals stay fully editable after creation — measure included
  • A met goal is archived or evolved; lineage stays visible as a chain
  • Quotes are the user’s own, added and managed in Options
  • Desktop content column widens to ~980px now the rail is gone

Still open

  • Activity: keep or drop the wall-clock day strip
  • How much of the pace band chart survives
  • Name: parked — the owner will revisit
  • Not yet drawn: mobile widths, the add-habit and add-quote forms

  • Pinned session zone replaces the scroll-away SessionBar

  • Quotes are your own — add them in Options, shown once on the first session of the day

Habits

Today, with everything one tap behind it

A flat list of boolean habits — the old routine steps, each standing on its own. The list shows only what’s due today; “Show all” reveals the rest, greyed. Every row ends the same way: the last seven occurrences as dots.

  • Frequency — Daily / Weekly / Every N days / set weekdays
  • Dots = last 7 occurrences, filled = done, ring = a day it wasn’t due
  • No time tracking on habits — that’s Goals’ job now
  • Open does a habit ever need an edit affordance inline, or menu-only

Goals

Cumulative, toward an outcome

Progress never resets. Each goal carries one measure — Time, Count, or Open — and any of them can also carry milestones. A focused goal shows its next action; banked focus time always counts, and shows as the bar for a Time goal or as context for the others.

  • Time — hours banked toward a target
  • Count — a number you name: km, steps, books, calls
  • Open — no number; milestones + a “done when” carry it
  • A goal must have a number or a milestone — else it’s a task

Pick how the goal is measured. Count reveals a unit you name and a target; Time asks for a number of hours; Open asks for nothing. Milestones are optional under all three.

  • Measure is changeable later — banked time is always kept
  • Evolves from links this goal to the one it succeeds
  • Open should the form block “Create” until there’s a number or a milestone

Goals

Met — archive or evolve

When a goal’s “done when” is checked, this is the choice. Evolving carries the history forward and keeps the chain visible on the new goal’s card.

  • Archive keeps the record, out of the active list
  • Evolve opens the New-goal form pre-linked to this one
