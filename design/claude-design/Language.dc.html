Instrument Panel

A calm, technical dashboard. Flat color blocks, hairline frames, grid paper and small mono readouts, borrowed from poster layouts and terminal setups. Color tells you which kind of time is running.

Amber and pale teal differ in hue and in warmth. Text on filled blocks is always ground ink, all pairs clear 4.5:1. A light theme swaps ground and paper and keeps the three signals.

JETBRAINS MONO, NUMBERS AND LABELS

SHAPE

  • Bento blocks. 28px outer, 22px tiles, 16px buttons.
  • Hairlines at 12% paper. Strong 45% for controls.
  • Grid paper at 7% inside the hero only.
  • One hero. Everything else supports it.
  • Bottom bar for views, thumb reach first.

MOTION / FAST, SPRINGY, NEVER BLOCKING

Press
Scale to 0.95, spring back with a small overshoot. 180ms.
Mode change
Hero floods to amber or teal. 500ms ease-out, ink flips with it.
Check off
Box fills, tick draws itself in 300ms, text dims and strikes.
Live dot
Slow 1.6s pulse while a timer runs. Still when resting.
Target ring
Fills smoothly each second. A small planet marks the center.
Reduced motion
Pulse and springs off. Color changes stay, instant.
